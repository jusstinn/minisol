## How it was tested

- **Browser.** Headless Chrome driven over the DevTools protocol. The driver, `scripts/qa/cdp.mjs`, records
  every `/api/*` call and each chat stream turn. Sizes used: 1440×900, 1280, 1024, 768, 390×844 and 360×780
  (touch emulation on for phone sizes), in RO and EN.
- **UI flows.** Every landing starter, all 7 project types, and the follow-ups from the brief: tiers, offers,
  stock, suggestions, sketch edits, undo, view commands and list commands.
- **Plan editor.** For every project type: drag a handle, open a "+" menu, click "×", use the quick controls,
  undo, check the change card. Also run at 390 px.
- **Other features.** On-demand sketch (`?retailer=hornbach`); list steppers (including rapid taps), options
  drawer, tiers, suggestions, store moves, cart (delivery and points), wallet modal, product sheet; saved
  sessions (reload, continue, keep chatting, forget); phone tabs, dots, `<canvas>` count and horizontal overflow.
- **Product mode.** `REQUIRE_PASS_LINK=1` with a test secret passed on the command line, and a minted link.
  Checked: the entry screen, chat, a 401 on every API call without the cookie, the wrong tenant, a tampered
  token, and a claimed `memberId`.
- **API robustness.** `scripts/qa/api-robustness.sh` plus large payloads: 10 MB message, 5 MB history,
  nesting 5,000 levels deep, 100k basket items, 10k edits.
- **Domain.** Calculators at typical and extreme sizes (0.5 m to 30 m decks, 1 m and 500 m fences, 1×1 rooms
  with 5 doors, 5,000 m² lawns). Pack selection read line by line. 90+ parser phrasings, with and without
  diacritics, `4x3`, `4 pe 3`, `4,5 m pe 3`, English. `scripts/qa/conversation.ts` replays whole
  conversations against the offline agent.

## Open — files I was told not to edit (suggested patches)

1. **[major] Requested deck height is ignored when the project is created** (`src/domain/layout.ts:196`).
   "deck 4 x 3, 0.5 m high" or "terasă ridicată la 50 cm" parse to `heightM: 0.5` (live model and offline),
   but `defaultLayout` hard-codes `heightM: 0.18`. The customer gets 60–100 mm pedestals and no ">60 cm
   railing" warning. Raising it afterwards via "ridic-o la 50 cm" works.
   ```ts
   heightM: typeof i.heightM === "number" ? Math.min(1.2, Math.max(0.1, i.heightM)) : 0.18,
   direction: i.direction === "z" ? "z" : "x",
   ```
2. **[minor] A new door always lands at `pos 0.5` on the default wall** (`layout.ts:528`, `:511` for gates).
   "adaugă o ușă" on a drywall wall that already has a door stacks a second one on top. Quantities count 2
   doors, so the price goes *down* 141.80 lei. Three doors on a 1 m wall are accepted (the calculator now
   rejects that, F21). The stacked doors also trigger React "two children with the same key `0:cw-210`" /
   `ghost:0:cw-210` errors from `src/components/blueprint/builders.ts:583` (`id: cw-${Math.round(x*100)}`).
   Fix: pick the widest free gap on the wall, or reject overlapping openings. Dedupe `xs` (or use the index)
   in the stud loop.
3. **[minor] Steps aren't checked against the shape** (`layout.ts:464`). On an L-shaped deck, "3 trepte în
   dreapta" puts steps on zone A's east edge, underneath zone B. Use `exposedEdges(l.zones)` to reject a side
   that isn't open. Minor detail: 6 steps on an 18 cm deck are accepted; cap the count at `ceil(heightM / 0.15)`.
4. **[minor] Measurement units in English** (`src/components/board/BlueprintPanel.tsx:281` and `:582`). They
   still show `{m.unit}`, so the EN sketch reads "11 buc" / "20 rânduri". Use `unitText(m.unit, lang)` from
   `@/lib/format`; MobileTabs and CardChips already do.
5. **[minor] Chat placeholder clipped on phones** (`src/components/workspace/Workspace.tsx:350`). "Întreabă
   orice, sau cere modificări…" wraps to 2 lines in a 1-line box at 390 px, with a scrollbar. Either
   shorten `placeholderFollow` in `src/lib/i18n.ts` (e.g. "Întreabă sau cere o modificare…") or add a short
   variant like the landing's `placeholderShort`.
6. **[minor] Two different "savings" figures.** The list says "Economisești 273,90 lei" (against the 30-day
   reference prices, Omnibus-safe), while the cart (`src/components/cart/CartDrawer.tsx`) lists discounts of
   129,90 + 150,00 = 279,90 lei. A picky customer will ask which is true. Show the same `saving`, or label
   the cart lines as "reduceri față de prețul de azi".
7. **[minor] Raw option keys shown to customers** (`layout.ts` set_option `say`). Quick controls and "fără
   tavan" produce "Setare actualizată: ceiling / floor / mode / pattern / direction" in the change card and
   replies. Map the keys to labels ("Fără tavan", "Montaj diagonal", "Reînsămânțare", "Deck pe lungime") in
   `say()`.
8. **[minor] Lawn area drifts** (`layout.ts:212-213`). "gazon 80 mp" becomes 79.98 m² in the sketch, the
   measurements and "Suprafață gazon". Don't round `d`: `const d = area / w;` (or r3).
9. **[polish] Plan editor accessibility** (`src/components/blueprint/PlanEditor.tsx:187-219`). The "+" `<g
   role="button" tabIndex={0}>` has no `onKeyDown` or `aria-label`. The "×" has neither `tabIndex` nor a
   label. The drag handles aren't reachable by keyboard. Add Enter/Space handlers and labels ("Adaugă pe
   latura de est", "Scoate zona B").
10. **[polish] Diff pill overlaps the legend** (`BlueprintPanel.tsx:333`). With the editor open the layer
    legend wraps to 2 rows, and "◆ 41 redimensionate" (`bottom-[58px]`) sits on top of it. Raise it when the
    dock is open, or place it top-left.
11. **[polish] "Scoate treptele" when there are none** (`layout.ts:478`). It answers "Gata — treptele au fost
    eliminate (±0,00 lei)". Throw "Nu există trepte" when `l.steps` is empty. Similarly, drywall doors are
    announced "pe peretele de sud" (the wall has no sides).
12. **[polish] Tier switch disappears after any hand change** (`src/lib/useAgent.ts`, reprice without
    `keepTiers`). After a single qty tap or option swap, the Economic/Standard/Premium comparison vanishes.
    Keep it, marked "calculat pentru lista inițială", or recompute it.
13. **[polish] Snap noise from the editor** (`PlanEditor.tsx:20`). The editor sends `resize w:
    6.1000000000000005`. `dim()` rounds it, but round in `snap` too: `Math.round(v / step) * step` →
    `+(...).toFixed(2)`.

## Not bugs / notes

- The first answer on desktop scrolls the board to the **plan** (the last card), so the 3D sketch and the
  list start off screen. This is by design ("scroll to the latest panel"), but it hides the hero content;
  see the UX ideas.
- Surfaces with no 3D (chat, list) have no `<canvas>`; the Schiță tab has exactly one. No hydration
  warnings or console errors in any flow, apart from item 2 above.
- Product mode behaves correctly: 401 without the cookie on all five APIs, a 303 with `pass=invalid` for a
  tampered, garbage or other-tenant token, the session cookie is httpOnly/lax, `Cache-Control: no-store`
  and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` are set, and a claimed `memberId` is ignored.
- Payload size is not capped. `/api/chat` accepted a 10 MB message (it is trimmed to 2,000 characters but
  still parsed in full). Consider a `content-length` guard (e.g. 413 above 2 MB) before `req.json()`.
- Quantities per m² are plausible for Romanian retail: larch deck ≈ 450 lei/m² including tools, laminate
  ≈ 90, bathroom ≈ 120, fence ≈ 300 lei/m, drywall ≈ 118 lei/m², lawn ≈ 15 lei/m².
- The WPC option keeps the decking oil; the bundle makes it free, but the plan says oil isn't needed for WPC.
- Quirks of the offline parser, left as they are: "pergolă cu acoperiș" triggers the roofing safety reply;
  "vopsesc gardul" starts a new fence; "best laminate" means premium; "vreau și un gard" replaces the deck
  (one project at a time).
- The `/pitch` `<title>` is English on the RO page.

## UX ideas

- On the first answer, scroll to the sketch (or pin a compact total and sketch header), not the plan.
- After a chat edit on a phone, a "Vezi pe schiță" chip under the reply. It exists for projects; add it
  for changes too.
- When "Da" can mean two things (store move and extras), ask them as two buttons.
- A "Recalculează nivelurile" link where the tier comparison used to be after hand edits.
- The change card could say *why* a price didn't move ("aceeași cutie de 10 l acoperă și fereastra").
- Step counts derived from height (17 cm rise) instead of a fixed 3; warn about railings at 60 cm in the
  edit reply, not only in the plan.
- Show "cel mult 30 m pe latură" as a hint in the sizes card, so out-of-range sizes are caught before asking.
- Cap title size on phones (4 lines of "Perete despărțitor din gips-carton" cover a third of the sketch):
  `text-[clamp(22px,…)]`.
- A cart note when a bundle gift doesn't apply to the chosen product (WPC + free oil).

## Tools added

- `scripts/qa/conversation.ts`: replay a conversation against the offline agent and print replies,
  cards and verified-amount badges.
  `npx tsx scripts/qa/conversation.ts WL-RO-100231 "Terasă 4 × 3 m" "adaugă 3 trepte în față" "Da"`
- `scripts/qa/cdp.mjs`: headless Chrome driver (Node 22+, no puppeteer) with API and stream recording,
  `chat()`, `idle()`, drag and screenshots.
- `scripts/qa/api-robustness.sh`: the malformed-input probes above. Every line must be a clean 2xx or 4xx.
